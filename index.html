How it's drawn

Low-res pass. The scene renders into a half-float target about 480×300 art pixels with a depth texture and no anti-aliasing.

Post pass. A shader reads each art pixel with texelFetch, tone-maps it, darkens pixels whose neighbour is more than 0.5 m nearer (depth outlines), then snaps the colour to the nearest of 68 palette entries in OKLab with lightness weighted ×1.35. Optional 4×4 Bayer dithering runs before the snap. A second pass upscales with whole-pixel texelFetch.

Stable pixels. The orthographic camera is nudged every frame so the world origin lands on a whole art pixel, so static geometry doesn't shimmer.

Glass and mesh. Glass is a small custom shader: tint, Fresnel-weighted sky reflection, edge highlights, faint floodlight glints and noise flecks, drawn without depth writes. Fencing is an alpha-tested grid texture. Walls facing the camera fade to about 20%.

People. Each player is about 21 boxes posed every frame: procedural gait, side shuffle, split step, and two-bone IK that drives the racket hand through prep → contact → follow-through.

Physics. A fixed 60 Hz tick with 6 substeps integrates gravity, quadratic drag and Magnus force. Floor, net and wall hits solve the exact crossing time inside a substep. Glass reflects with restitution 0.72; mesh absorbs more and adds a deterministic tangential kick.

AI. A predictor clones the ball and runs the same physics forward, logging bounces and wall hits. Each player scores reachable contact windows (travel time, reaction, height, rule legality); the best one intercepts while the partner covers. Shots are solved by bisecting launch speed per candidate angle until the landing point matches a target and the net is cleared, then aim error is added.

Rules. Contacts are judged from physics events only: serve box, lets, faults, wall-before-bounce, second bounce, body hits and balls leaving the cage.

WebGL2 needed

Glass Court renders with WebGL2, which this browser or device doesn't provide. Try a recent Chrome, Firefox, Safari or Edge with hardware acceleration turned on.
